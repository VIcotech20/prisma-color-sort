DESCRIPTION (EN)
PRISMA is a hybrid-casual color sort. Drag pigment from vial to vial, keep a combo, and cork each flask when the spectrum is solved. 200 levels, a daily challenge, Eclipse vials that hide their color, and boosters (undo, hint, extra vial). Play in the browser. No account.
